How sharing works
  • Each paper is in one shared place: New Papers, My Collection or Trash. The last move counts.
  • When someone moves a paper (collects it, trashes it, also from My Collection, or puts it back), it stays in everyone else's New Papers, marked with who did it, until they keep it there or move it themselves.
  • Disagree? Move it again, e.g. collect a paper someone trashed. They then find your move in their New Papers.
